📌 How to connect your domain:
  1. Open your domain provider (e.g. Cloudflare, Namecheap, GoDaddy, Hostinger, Porkbun).
  2. Navigate to the DNS Records / DNS Management page for your domain.
  3. Delete any existing conflicting A or CNAME records for @ and www.
  4. Add the two A records shown in the table above pointing to {{ server_ip }}.
  5. Cloudflare note: Set the proxy status to DNS Only (Grey Cloud) when issuing SSL certificates.
  6. Save the records. Most providers propagate within 5–15 minutes!
